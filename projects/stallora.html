Stallora

2026 Next.js · Supabase · Stripe
GitHub ↗

Stallora is a simple storefront for small sellers — home bakers, vintage shops, handmade businesses, student sellers, local vendors — who need to upload a few products, share one link, and accept payments. Not a smaller Shopify.

How it works

  • Sellers get a single shareable page for their products
  • Stripe handles checkout; Supabase handles auth and data
  • The hard part is deciding what not to build so the product stays simple
