Rubber Duck Learning

2026 Rust · Tauri
GitHub ↗

A desktop rubber duck for developers who want help thinking through code. A movable duck icon stays on your screen. Click it when you are stuck, paste an error or describe the bug, and it responds with Socratic questions, file-reading prompts, and small hints instead of dumping the solution.

Why

Most AI coding tools optimize for speed. Rubber Duck Learning optimizes for understanding. It helps you debug, trace, and reason through code while keeping you in control.
